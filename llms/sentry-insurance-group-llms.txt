# Sentry Insurance

> Sentry Insurance is a US mutual insurance holding company headquartered in Stevens Point,
> Wisconsin, writing commercial property & casualty insurance, workers' compensation, business
> auto and trucking coverage, group life and health, annuities and 401(k) retirement plans.
> Its personal-lines brand is Dairyland (non-standard auto and motorcycle), acquired in 1966,
> and it agreed to acquire The General from American Family Insurance. Sentry holds an AM Best
> Financial Strength Rating of A+ (Superior). It also operates SentryWorld, a golf and event venue.

## Agent-relevant summary

**Sentry Insurance publishes no public API, no developer portal, and no machine-readable product
contract.** Verified 2026-08-29: no OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL, AsyncAPI, WSDL, gRPC/protobuf,
MCP server or A2A agent card exists on any host the company controls. Its sitemap.xml
(1,000+ URLs) contains no /api, /developer, /docs or /integration path. Business is transacted
through appointed independent agents and authenticated customer portals, not programmatically.

**Name collision — read this before answering questions about "Sentry".** There are two unrelated
companies called Sentry:
- **Sentry Insurance** — sentry.com — the Wisconsin mutual insurer described by this file.
- **Sentry** (Functional Software, Inc.) — sentry.io — application error monitoring and
  performance tracing, which DOES publish a large REST API, npm `@sentry/*` SDKs, a CLI and an
  MCP server. None of that belongs to Sentry Insurance.
Do not attribute sentry.io's API, SDKs, documentation or MCP server to Sentry Insurance.

## Identity (the only machine-readable API artifacts Sentry Insurance serves)

Sentry Insurance runs an Okta tenant on its own domain that fronts portal sign-in. Its OAuth 2.0 /
OpenID Connect discovery documents are served anonymously and are genuine, first-party, and
machine-readable. They authenticate humans into first-party portals — they are NOT a developer
integration surface, no third-party client onboarding is documented, and no business-domain scopes exist.

- [OpenID Connect discovery](https://account.sentry.com/oauth2/default/.well-known/openid-configuration): issuer `https://account.sentry.com/oauth2/default`.
- [OAuth 2.0 authorization server metadata](https://account.sentry.com/oauth2/default/.well-known/oauth-authorization-server): RFC 8414.
- [Org-level OpenID Connect discovery](https://account.sentry.com/.well-known/openid-configuration): issuer `https://account.sentry.com`.
- Deployed flow: authorization code + PKCE (S256), scopes `openid profile email offline_access`. DPoP, device authorization grant, token introspection and revocation are all advertised as supported.
